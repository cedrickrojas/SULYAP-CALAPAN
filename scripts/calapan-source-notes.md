# Calapan source review — October 8, 2026

The client supplied https://www.travelorientalmindoro.ph/ as a destination-information resource. The app keeps the ten client-selected places and stable IDs. Descriptions are paraphrased, and source links appear in the existing information pages.

## Accepted listing details

- Silonay: 42-hectare mangrove site, 350-meter boardwalk, six-meter viewing tower; biodiversity counts explicitly attributed to the historical 2014 survey. Listed phone `0920 287 5330`, free admission, and map coordinates `13.40094506, 121.2251015`.
- Heritage Museum: use `/place/oriental-mindoro-heritage-museum`, the Old Provincial Capitol / Cal-kuta site in Ibaba East. MAHAL TANA themes, listed free admission, museum email `orientalmindoroheritagemuseum@gmail.com`, and coordinates `13.41496409, 121.1801701`.
- Zoological and Recreational Park: the portal lists this Bulusan site as Calapan Nature Park. Tree-shaded grounds, mini-zoo, camping/picnic and climbing facilities, and Bulusan eco-trail toward Parang are described as facilities whose current access should be checked. Phone `(043) 288-3883`, listed free admission, coordinates `13.40496178, 121.1991656`.
- Plaza del Gobernador: Gov. Ignacio Street, Camilmil; fountain, seating, playground, and local stalls. Listed free admission, phone `0967 082 9736`, email `plazadelgobernador@gmail.com`, and coordinates `13.40518677, 121.1769897`.
- Cathedral: the existing Ibaba East address, parish phone, free-admission description, and checked cathedral-area coordinates remain.
- Tourism offices: `/directory/tourism-offices` lists the Calapan office at the Heritage / Culture and Arts Building, Bonifacio Drive, Ibaba East; phone `0967 233 6074`, email `calapancitytourism@gmail.com`. This is the clearly named assistance contact for sites without direct confirmed contacts.
- `/page/how-to-get-here` describes ferries from Batangas to Calapan and RORO carriage of vehicles. The guide uses that approach without guaranteeing timetables, operators, fares, or journey duration.

The accepted map pins are published destination reference locations, not independent surveys of current entrances. The app retains entrance-confirmation notes. Admission wording says what the portal lists and separates any activity or service charges.

## Source discrepancies and gaps

- The cathedral listing's directions coordinate (`13.37823046, 121.1651202`) differs substantially from its Ibaba East address and the existing checked cathedral location; it was not adopted.
- The cathedral and Silonay pages contain the same unrelated-looking Mangyan cultural-booth email; it was not assigned as a direct contact for either destination.
- `/place/oriental-mindoro-heritage-museum-1` describes the City Museum at New City Hall. Its address, contacts, and gallery are not used for the requested Old Provincial Capitol Heritage Museum.
- The portal footer's `(043) 123-4567` is not used as a tourism-office contact.
- No matching individual listings were found for Aganhao Islet, Caluangan Lake, City Plaza, Baco Island, or Suqui Beach. Existing place-specific sources remain. The resort listing in Suqui is not treated as the public beach's entry fee, contact, or visitor entrance. Baco municipality is not substituted for Baco Island.
- Detailed current visitor hours are absent from the reviewed listings. The pre-existing March 2026 museum and fountain advisories remain dated and qualified; other unknown hours and fees are left for confirmation.

## Expanded profiles: Aganhao, Caluangan, and Suqui

The three profiles now include destination-specific descriptions, arrival steps, activities, attractions, visit planning, rate questions, and travel tips within their existing information sections. Routes, IDs, map behavior, QR links, and galleries are preserved.

- Aganhao: the historical Vigattin Calapan guide describes a white-sand strip near Silonay Island. The September 2014 first-hand Tripadvisor account supplies rocky-shore context and the Suqui/Lazareto boat approaches, presented as historical approaches requiring operator confirmation. Nearby Harka Piloto has a separate Department of Tourism listing; its hours, fees, contact number, and water activities are not copied onto Aganhao. No current landing permission, ferry timetable, tariff, facilities, or swimming suitability is claimed.
- Caluangan: PIA's September 28, 2022 report documents the SMARTT floating cottages in Tawagan and group lake/river outings with food. The profile explicitly dates that report and asks visitors to verify current operation. The October 10, 2022 PIA report identifies the five shoreline communities and bamboo-planting activity. The city government's February 6, 2026 aquaculture report describes oyster-culture pilot plans, not a tourist activity already operating. Historical craft capacity and proposed lake-development facilities are not represented as current services. Conflicting online lake-area, formation-date, and depth figures are omitted.
- Suqui: the historical Calapan tourism guide identifies the gray-sand Lazareto–Suqui–Parang coast. First-hand 2014 beach reviews add dark-sand and local-picnic context and distinguish the barangay shore from private beachfront property. Provincial coastal-clean-up records confirm the Suqui location. Current entry prices, cottages, lifeguards, opening hours, and swimming conditions remain for local confirmation; old review prices are not reused.

Each profile retains the verified Calapan City Tourism Office assistance contact and links its source pages in Further reading. Practical travel tips are planning recommendations, not quoted municipal regulations. The current request concerns destination details; existing missing-photo labels remain accurate.
